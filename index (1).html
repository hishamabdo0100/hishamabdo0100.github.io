Featured ProjectsRecent work

Customer Churn Prediction

Machine Learning

A model that predicts which bank customers are likely to leave, so a retention team knows who to contact first.

Problem
Finding customers at risk of leaving before they go, in data where only 1 in 5 customers leaves.
Approach
Engineered new features, compared Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost and LightGBM, tuned them with Optuna while controlling overfitting, and explained the predictions with SHAP.
Result
ROC-AUC of 0.87 on unseen customers. It finds about two thirds of the customers who leave (65% recall, 64% precision).
Python XGBoost LightGBM Optuna SHAP Scikit-learn

Spam Email Detection

NLP / Machine Learning

A classifier that separates spam from legitimate email and shows which words give spam away.

Problem
Filtering unwanted email automatically without blocking real messages.
Approach
Cleaned and de-duplicated 5,728 emails, built TF-IDF features, and compared three models tuned with GridSearchCV.
Result
99.56% accuracy on unseen emails: 5 mistakes out of 1,139.
Python NLP TF-IDF Scikit-learn

Movie Review Sentiment Analysis

NLP / Deep Learning

A sentiment classifier for movie reviews that compares a classic baseline with a fine-tuned BERT model.

Problem
Understanding audience sentiment across 50,000 written reviews without reading them by hand.
Approach
Built a TF-IDF and Logistic Regression baseline, then fine-tuned BERT with PyTorch and Hugging Face.
Result
About 92% accuracy with BERT, against 88.8% for the baseline.
Python NLP BERT PyTorch Hugging Face
